Kids Store — Black Friday FOOH (spec ad)

This is a 14.5-second, 9:16 spec ad for a fictional kids’ store. One Black Friday poster comes alive: an original robot leans out, climbs through its frame as if stepping through a window, jumps down to the street, points toward the store window, and the film ends on the card.

The poster frame sets up one clear reveal; the robot’s jump carries the action into the street before its point toward the store window and the closing Black Friday card.

Format: 14.5 s · vertical 9:16 Project: Spec ad · fictional store Visual: One poster · robot lands in street · store window · end card

Selected production frames

A single poster reveal carries the action from the building into the street, then points the viewer toward the store window. These frames are taken from the finished v3 film.

Brief & approach

Create a vertical Black Friday FOOH spec film for a fictional kids’ store: make one building poster feel like a window, bring an original robot through it, then land the action on the shop and offer. The retired client is not involved; this is not presented as commissioned work or an endorsement.

Tools used

  • FLUX and FLUX Klein — robot and keyframe edits
  • kz_text.py — campaign typography on the frames
  • H3 image-to-video — six shot animations
  • kz_comp.py and ACE-Step — edit, sound effects, end card and music

Turnaround

Same-day spec production, 25 Nov 2026 (the archived project record dates the v3 build and handoff to that day). Final cut: 14.5 seconds, vertical 9:16.

Disclosure

Spec piece for a fictional store. No client relationship, commission, or endorsement is claimed. The two music versions are alternate edits of the same film.
