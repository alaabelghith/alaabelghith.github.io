Kids Store — Black Friday FOOH (spec ad)

This is a 14.5-second, 9:16 spec ad for a fictional kids’ store. One Black Friday poster comes alive: an original robot leans out, climbs through its frame as if stepping through a window, jumps down to the street, points toward the store window, and the film ends on the card.

The poster frame sets up one clear reveal; the robot’s jump carries the action into the street before its point toward the store window and the closing Black Friday card.

Format: 14.5 s · vertical 9:16 Project: Spec ad · fictional store Visual: One poster · robot lands in street · store window · end card
