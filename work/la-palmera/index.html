La Palmera

A launch campaign written as a creative test for La Palmera, a new nightclub in Saint-Jean-d’Angély, France, opening on 5 December. The club has three rooms, so the idea is one night, three worlds: the neon palm of the logo is the hero, and each room gets its own colour world taken from the club’s palettes.

Delivered as a launch poster, an Instagram story, three room cards and a 15-second teaser in vertical and 4:5, with a publishing plan for the weeks before opening. Backgrounds, video clips and music made with open AI models on my own workstation; layout, type and motion finished by hand. The logo belongs to the club; shown here as a pitch, not a commissioned job.

Role: Concept, art direction, graphic design, motion, edit Deliverables: Poster 4:5 · story 9:16 · 3 room cards · 15 s teaser (9:16, 4:5) Stack: Open image, video and music models on a local GPU · Python layout · ffmpeg Status: Pitch, not commissioned
