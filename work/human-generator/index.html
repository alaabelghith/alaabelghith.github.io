Human Generator

A Houdini tool that turns research models into one production node. It builds a human body in real units, age in years, height in cm and weight in kg, and solves the body to hit those numbers exactly, from a 6-year-old child to an 80-year-old man. Gender, muscle, breasts, buttocks and a volume-keeping surface smooth shape it further.

Type a sentence and NVIDIA’s Kimodo model animates the body, on my own GPU, inside Houdini. Wire a curve into the node and Kimodo generates the motion on that path: in the film he runs along a curve I drew and finishes with a gymnast’s forward roll. Next: textures, then crowds.

Role: Concept and direction, Houdini integration, AI-assisted build Stack: Houdini Python SOP · NVIDIA Kimodo · SOMA and Anny body models · local GPU Status: Free and open source, v1.0 (Apache-2.0)
