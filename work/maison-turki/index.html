Maison Turki

An unsolicited concept for Maison Turki, a Tunisian pastry house. An AI pipeline isolated 421 individual pastries from the shop’s catalogue photos, and an interactive prototype lets customers fill any gift box piece by piece before ordering.

Code on GitHub: compose-ta-box.

Compose ta Box explores a gift-box selection flow: choose a box, then add products to its sections. A cutout pipeline produced 421 single-piece pastry images from catalogue photography, and a browser prototype presents the selection interaction. Visitors can preview a combination before deciding what to request. A future shop integration could connect an approved product catalogue to the seller’s existing ordering flow. A comparable brief can define box options, product choices, allowed combinations and the handoff from an approved basket to order processing. The prototype focuses on presenting and arranging products inside a box, giving the concept a clear path from visual selection to a future shop integration. The prototype offers 16 box formats and lets visitors fill sections piece by piece from a filtered product catalogue.

Role: Concept, AI cutout pipeline, prototype direction Status: Pitch, not commissioned

Frequently asked questions

What does Compose ta Box demonstrate?

Compose ta Box presents a gift-box selection concept: choose a box, then fill its sections piece by piece from a filtered product catalogue. It is a pitch prototype, not an official shop product or a live checkout. The interaction explores product presentation before any approved order-system integration.

How many product cutouts were made for the prototype?

The cutout pipeline produced 421 single-piece pastry images from catalogue photography. Those images support the selection flow, where a visitor chooses a box and adds products to its sections. The prototype offers 16 box formats and lets a visitor preview a gift-box combination before sending a request to the seller.

Does the prototype take or process an order?

No. It is a browser prototype for choosing a box and placing products into its sections. It does not take payment or connect to inventory or order processing. A production version could use the seller’s approved catalogue and pass a selected basket into the existing ordering system.
