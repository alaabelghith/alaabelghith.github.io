Process

How a project runs

From the first conversation to final delivery.

1. Consultation First

Every project begins with understanding. I dig into your intent, audience, context and desired outcome. The consultation is always free, and it is how I work out the right approach for your project.

  • Understand project goals and constraints
  • Explore visual direction and references
  • Determine optimal production approach
  • Establish timeline and deliverables

2. Production Approaches

AI-Driven

Leverage cutting-edge AI tools for rapid iteration and exploration. Ideal for conceptual work, style frames, and quick turnaround projects.

Best for: Concept exploration, rapid prototyping, social content

Hybrid AI + 3D

Combine AI generation with traditional 3D workflows for enhanced control and refinement. Balanced approach for most commercial projects.

Best for: Marketing content, product visualization, branded content

Full CGI / VFX

Complete control through traditional 3D modeling, rendering, and compositing. Maximum precision for simulation-heavy and high-end commercial work.

Best for: FX shots, product launches, broadcast quality

3. Delivery & Iteration

01

Initial Delivery

First draft delivered based on consultation and creative brief. Includes all primary deliverables in working resolution.

02

Feedback Round

A structured session to review the work and identify refinements. Detailed notes and clear direction are welcome — they make the next round faster.

03

Refinement

Implement feedback and polish details. Standard projects include two rounds of revisions.

04

Final Delivery

Complete package with all final assets in requested formats, resolutions, and specifications. Includes source files when applicable.

About

Alaa Belghith

I'm a Houdini FX and 3D artist with six years in production. I specialise in simulation-heavy work — pyro, FLIP, RBD, cloth, crowds — alongside rigging, product CGI and AI-assisted video. Anything Visual is the name I work under as a freelancer.

Most recently I was 3D generalist and rigger at CGS on the feature To Be Italian, broadcast on Rai 3, followed by freelance product and skincare work for studio clients. Before that I was a 3D generalist at Voxel Studio and animated on the 2D series Wled El Bhar.

I hold a Master in VFX from FX Animation Barcelona 3D & Film School and a Licence in animation from ESAD Tunis (mention Très Bien). I also build and publish my own Houdini tools, written in VEX. I work in Arabic, French, English and Spanish.
