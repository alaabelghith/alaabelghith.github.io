World Window: a head-tracked 3D window

World Window is an interactive experiment by Anything Visual. Move the mouse to look around, scroll to lean in, or enable the webcam for head-tracked off-axis projection. The browser demo uses Three.js and MediaPipe and includes a Sidi Bou Said scene. Contact Alaa Belghith at belguithalaa@gmail.com.

World Window explores how a fixed screen can behave like a window into a three-dimensional scene. Move the mouse to shift the viewer position, scroll to lean closer or farther away, or enable the webcam for head tracking. The projection keeps the scene aligned with the screen edges while nearby and distant elements move against each other. The Sidi Bou Said at dusk scene is built in code rather than loaded from a texture or model. Webcam use is optional; mouse control remains available without a camera. Choose the screen size and camera position for an installation, then explore the perspective from the intended viewing distance. Webcam tracking uses the MediaPipe Face Landmarker to follow nose position and estimate screen distance from the eyes.
